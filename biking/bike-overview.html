5  Bike overview and equipment

General anatomy notes and the toolkit required for routine maintenance.

5.1 Essential toolkit

Figure 5.1 is the published contents of the Decathlon 900 box: (6–11 speed), hex keys, Torx T10–T30, , Shimano HG , and a nut wrench. Check the physical box before buying extras — the listed hex set often stops at 8 mm.

Buy in addition: a 10 mm hex and a that reaches at least 41 Nm. Quick-link pliers and grease are comfort, not strict necessities.

5.1.1 Torque wrench note

For an FSA 30 mm axle with a self-extracting bolt, FSA specifies a 10 mm hex and 38–41 Nm with a calibrated wrench.

A compact 4–24 Nm wrench with a 10 mm bit is excellent for derailleurs and chainring bolts but does not reach crank-bolt torque. Prefer a 3/8″ or 1/4″+3/8″ wrench covering roughly 5–50 Nm or 5–60 Nm with a 10 mm hex bit.
