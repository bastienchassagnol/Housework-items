6.2 How to monitor rim brakes

  1. Pad thickness — replace before the metal holder contacts the rim.
  2. Pad alignment — toe-in slightly at the leading edge; full contact without rubbing the tyre.
  3. Rim wear — look for a wear groove disappearing or a concave profile.
  4. Cable and housing — smooth lever travel; no fraying at the end.
  5. Quick test — wheel spins freely off the stand; firm lever feel with ~2–3 cm travel before firm contact.
